Compatibility Note

For Samsung Exynos devices on stock or custom ROMs. Snapdragon variants -- especially most US and Canadian models -- are not supported.

01

Check system compatibility

Ensure your target Samsung device is powered by an Exynos SoC with root access established.

02

Review official instructions

Follow the README located at sofikuw/MOSS for exact flashing and configuration steps.

03

Flash via Magisk or KernelSU

Install the module ZIP inside your manager app and verify image updates upon reboot.

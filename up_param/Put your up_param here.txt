Put your file here and name it exactly:

    up_param.bin

The file must be an uncompressed TAR archive containing your splash JPEGs
(logo.jpg, lpm.jpg, download.jpg, ...). Do not zip it and do not use PNG.

You can create it with:

    sh tools/pack_up_param.sh /sdcard/my_splash /sdcard/up_param.bin

Then copy /sdcard/up_param.bin into this folder and flash the ZIP from a
TWRP-based recovery, Magisk, KernelSU, or APatch.
